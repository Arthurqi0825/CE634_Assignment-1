Key readout

What the analysis says

Pooling is mostly an Uber signal

Uber has materially higher pooling request and reported match rates than Lyft in both months.

Shared rides add travel time

Supported Uber shared trips show median excess travel time of about 4.5 to 5.0 minutes.

Shared fares are lower; many N/N trips cluster

Supported Y/Y trips are cheaper than comparable N/N reference cells, and many N/N trips have same-OD temporal neighbors.

How to read the evidence

Observed completed trips

Counts describe recorded trips in TLC files, not unique people, all app requests, or causal demand.

Comparable cells

Shared-trip time and fare comparisons use matched OD-time reference cells and report support coverage.

Potential sharing

Task 5.2 is a zone-time co-occurrence screen, not a platform dispatch simulation or guaranteed match.
